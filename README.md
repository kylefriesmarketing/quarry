# QUARRY

**▶ Play: https://kylefriesmarketing.github.io/quarry/**

A first-person alien hunting simulator. Browser, vanilla JS + Three.js.
*Dirty Boy Devs — a game of THE ROOM.*

---

You are a hunter of an old and serious people, and you have come a very long way to find
something worth killing.

Your technology makes hunting trivial, and **using it is what costs you.** Two currencies
pull in opposite directions for the whole life of the game: **bounty** pays for the carcass,
**honor** pays for the method. The cloaked kill from a ridge is the best money and the worst
standing — at every rarity tier.

**You cannot die. You can be broken.** Lose badly and you wake on your ship, injured, and the
beast keeps a piece of your kit and stays on that world wearing it. Nothing rewinds.

**Every world runs on a single biological law** that every organism on it obeys — *nothing
here touches the ground*, *everything wears the ground it eats*, *nothing runs a temperature*.
The species, their bodies, their voices and their food web are generated under it.

## Controls

| | |
|---|---|
| **W A S D** | move |
| **Shift** | slow · **Ctrl** crouch |
| **Mouse** | look |
| **1 / 2 / 3** | optics: none · thermal · pheromone |
| **F** | throw spear |
| **E** | rest & skip to the next phase (only when still) |
| **C** | cloak — it will cost you |
| **Esc** | release the mouse |

Add **`?seed=3`** to the URL for a different world. Every seed is a different biological law,
a different biome and a different roster of animals. Try `?seed=3` against `?seed=7`.

## Reading the hunt

Three senses, three counters. **Sight** is beaten by cover and stillness. **Sound** is beaten
by moving slowly and choosing your ground. **Scent** is beaten by wind discipline — the arrow
at the top of the screen points where your scent is going, and upwind of an animal you are
invisible to its nose.

The bars bottom-left are what the animal can currently perceive of you. Read them.

Assess before you commit: the readout top-right tells you the specimen's quality at range, and
the **pheromone optic is the only way to tell whether she is gravid.** Killing a gravid female
voids the trophy. You were told.

## The ship

Press **Tab** (or click *RETURN TO SHIP*) to go up to orbit. From there you get a star map of
your sector, a fuel gauge, and your charts.

**Fuel is bought with bounty, and bounty comes off the carcass.** A cloaked kill from a ridge
pays well and scores nothing; an honest fight scores well and pays almost nothing. So the
hunter who plays for honor is the hunter who gets stranded — that is the game, not a bug.

Every world you visit is written into the **Field Journal**: its law, its water and bedding
grounds, what you killed there, and anything that got away carrying a piece of your kit. A
world you have been to can be plotted back to. Worlds you have never seen are UNCHARTED and
show only what a jump to them would cost.

`?newgame` wipes your save and starts a fresh galaxy.
